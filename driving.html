GO starts assisted driving. The car follows the road, carries speed through broad bends, and brakes ahead of tighter turns based on available grip. Hold DRIFT or the space bar to slide. Use left or right while drifting to choose the direction; without steering, it follows the corner. Apex Academy has a wider road with a sequence of teaching corners. The amber line shows the outside entry, blue marks the inside apex, and green leads toward the outside exit. Choose Show me the line to watch, or Let me steer to practice moving across the road with the arrow buttons. Forward travel and cornering remain assisted. This is a teaching line, not a professional lap-time simulation. Pause whenever you like.

Build my track makes a saved copy of the course you are playing. If you already have a custom track, it opens that creation. Tap the numbered road dots or use the piece selector. Every edit keeps a closed loop. Undo restores the previous edit. Racing and building are free, and laps do not create money.

Interior dashcam view is mounted inside the windshield and faces Artin and Leo’s 3D avatars. Corkscrew Canyon is an original course inspired by the hills and downhill turns of Laguna Seca.
