GO starts assisted driving. The car follows the road and slows for corners. Hold DRIFT or the space bar to slide. Use left or right while drifting to choose the direction; without steering, it follows the corner. Arrow keys gently move within the lane. Pause whenever you like.

Build my track makes a saved copy of the course you are playing. If you already have a custom track, it opens that creation. Tap the numbered road dots or use the piece selector. Every edit keeps a closed loop. Undo restores the previous edit. Racing and building are free, and laps do not create money.

The original coupe is inspired by 1990s Japanese sports cars. Its synthesized turbo straight-six sound is an interpretation, not a Nissan or movie recording. Corkscrew Canyon is an original course inspired by the hills and downhill turns of Laguna Seca. Chillion Bucks is not affiliated with Nissan or the raceway.
