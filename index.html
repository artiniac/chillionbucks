👨‍👩‍👦 For grown-ups

What this site is, and what it is not.

Free. No ads. No accounts.

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to buy, nothing collected. Worlds, savings, goals, and preferences stay in this browser on this device.

Where the videos come from

Every video is a real YouTube video from a teacher or a kids’ channel, hand-picked and embedded with YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. If one goes stale, it gets swapped.

How to use it with your kid

  • Play Need or Want together and argue about pizza.
  • Pick a goal, then use Piggy Bank Power to deposit earned paychecks.
  • Slide the Chill-o-Meter and let them see the “baby money” number jump.

Chilltopia 3D and the classic builder

The new 3D aquarium and town save on this device only. The aquarium is a creative reef sandbox, not real fish-care advice. Jobs earn pretend bills that must be deposited before spending. There are no purchases or uploads. Existing classic worlds remain available in the classic builder.

The classic builder can use a photo of your child’s room as the background. The photo is resized and kept only in this browser on this device (it never leaves the phone or computer, and there is no server to send it to). “New world” or clearing the browser’s site data removes it. Finished worlds are saved as small pictures on the same device only.

In the classic builder, every snap-together structure piece is free (blocks, magnet tiles, walls, roofs, roads, coaster track, river channels, castle walls, garage decks). Pools, rides, cars, and characters cost bucks. Bucks come from work: the Job Board has quick jobs (vacuum the room, clean the pool, wash a car, paint a fence, deliver packages, feed the animals, run the lemonade stand) that pay a bill, and build orders that pay a bill when a build is complete. Every bill has to be dragged into the piggy before it can be spent. Classic jobs pay 5 pretend bucks, up to two paid jobs per day, within the shared daily limit of 45 bucks. Aquarium feeding pays 5, cleanup pays 10, deliveries pay 10, and water-park care pays 10. Existing savings remain yours. Free coins are practice only. There are no streak penalties.

One-tap goals from a store app. iPhone: open the Shortcuts app, tap +, name it “Add to Chillion Bucks”, tap the ⓘ and turn on “Show in Share Sheet” (accept URLs and Text). Add three actions: “URL Encode” (Shortcut Input), then “Text” containing https://chillionbucks.com/?text= followed by the URL Encoded Text, then “Open URLs”. Now in the Amazon app (or any store), tap Share on a toy, tap “Add to Chillion Bucks”, and this page opens with the link and name filled in; you check the price and tap Add. Android: open chillionbucks.com in Chrome, tap “Install app” once, and “Chillion Bucks” appears directly in the Share sheet.

The savings goals are real things. Under the goal cards, “Grown-ups: add a real goal” takes a store link, a name, a price, and (optionally) the product photo’s address, and the goal appears as a picture card on this device only. When the piggy reaches the price, the kid makes a certificate to show you, and the store button (labeled for grown-ups, opens in a new tab) takes you to the listing. Nothing on this site takes money, tracks anyone, or reads anything from other sites.
