# Chillion Bucks: care, work, and saving

Implemented September 7, 2026. These are initial game-design choices informed by research, not clinically validated reward amounts or a promise of habit formation.

## Play loop

Choose a useful task, finish it, collect a predictable paycheck, deposit it in the piggy, and decide whether to save or buy something. Free construction remains available without doing a job. No missed-day penalties, fish deaths, escalating jackpots, streak resets, or paid purchases.

| Task | Pay in pretend bucks | Paid opportunities per local calendar day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feed the aquarium | 5 | 1, three small portions with a pause between portions |
| Clean the aquarium | 10 | 1, collect ten litter pieces |
| Town deliveries | 10 | 2, deliver four packages per trip |
| Water-park filters | 10 | 1, clear six filter baskets |
| Classic work, orders, or finished worlds | 5 | 2 total across Classic activities |

The shared ceiling is 45 bucks per day. Classic jobs use the same allowance, not an extra allowance. Pay arrives in 5-buck bills and requires an explicit deposit. Current savings and previously earned bills are preserved. Care completion remains recorded across reloads and world changes. Modern browsers use Web Locks to serialize simultaneous payment claims across tabs. Progress is local to this browser, not synced across devices. This is not an adversarial or real-money economy: changing the device clock, editing browser storage, or clearing it can defeat on-device limits.

The 4 to 15 buck fish and crawler prices give an early reachable goal. A 25 buck castle takes several tasks. Structural park pieces are free so imagination is never blocked by the pay cap. The maximum is a ceiling, not a recommended daily workload. Children can stop after any task.

Unlimited home-page coins now practice depositing without increasing spendable savings. Waiting at a temptation prompt no longer creates a bonus buck. The former automatic daily wallet growth and large milestone payouts are removed. The separate 7% annual growth simulator remains explicitly a game assumption, not a forecast.

## Research and interpretation

- [Self-Determination Theory: intrinsic motivation](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/topics/application-intrinsic-motivation/). Meaningful choice and useful competence feedback can support motivation. Application here: optional jobs, free creative play, visible outcomes, and feedback about the action completed. Bucks are an extrinsic reward; we should not claim they automatically create intrinsic interest.
- [Morris and Zentall, 2014: High fives motivate](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2014.00928/full). This experiment with young children examined praise and subsequent motivation. It supports paying attention to the form of feedback, but does not establish a payout schedule for games. Application: acknowledge specific completed work instead of labeling a child smart or good.
- [Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts, and Wardle, 2010: How are habits formed?](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/ejsp.674). Repetition in a consistent context was studied in adults, with substantial variation in habit formation. Missing one opportunity did not materially derail the process in that study. Application: recognizable care routines and forgiving returns. Adult findings do not establish how long a child's gaming routine should take to form.

No source establishes that 5 or 10 bucks is ideal. These amounts are a transparent balancing hypothesis. Parent-observed playtesting should assess whether Leo understands earning versus depositing, enjoys the task itself, can name a savings goal, and voluntarily returns to creative play after work. If earning feels tedious, improve the task or adjust prices before increasing rewards. No behavioral analytics or personal data are collected.

## Aquarium learning boundary

Crayfish are freshwater animals, as illustrated by the [U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service species description](https://www.fws.gov/species/big-sandy-crayfish-cambarus-callainus). The collection is explicitly a make-believe aquarium, not advice to house freshwater crayfish with marine fish, lobsters, or crabs. There is no real feeding-volume recommendation or life-support simulation.
