# Claude Code rules for Chillion Bucks

Read before touching anything in this repo.

## What this is
Leo's (age almost 5) money site for kids, chillionbucks.com. "A chillion bucks is when you can chill all day and still have a lot of money." Owner: Artin Nazaryan (Leo's dad). Goal: make kids curious about money through games and good videos, and look genuinely cool while doing it.

## Hard rules
1. **Zero cost, forever.** Static files only (HTML, CSS, vanilla JS). No frameworks, no build step, no npm dependencies, no backend, no databases, no paid APIs, no analytics services. Hosting is GitHub Pages. If a feature needs a server, it does not ship here.
2. **Video comes from YouTube only**, via `videos.js` and the privacy-enhanced embed (`youtube-nocookie.com`). Never host video files. Every id added must pass `node verify-videos.mjs` (it hits YouTube's oEmbed endpoint) before it is committed. Never invent an id from memory.
3. **Kid-safe.** Audience is roughly ages 4 to 9. No accounts, no forms that collect anything, no tracking, no ads. External links: the YouTube creators' own videos, and the savings-goal store links from `goals.js`, which render ONLY behind a button labeled for a grown-up, open in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and never auto-open (Artin 2026-09-05: real store listings so a kid who cannot read can pick what to save for). `localStorage` holds only on-device game state: sound on/off (`cb:sound`), the wallet with its earned bills (`cb:wallet`), the Builder world (`cb:world`, which may include a downscaled room photo as a data URL), the gallery of finished worlds (`cb:worlds`, capped at 12, each with a small JPEG thumbnail), job cooldowns and counts (`cb:work`), grown-up-added goals (`cb:goals`: name, price, store link, photo link), whether a goal was ever picked (`cb:goalPicked`), the kid's first name for the certificate (`cb:kidName`), the coin count (`cb:coins`), and the last badge letter a kid typed into the pal maker (`cb:palLetter`, one character). The site NEVER reads cookies, history, or anything from another site; Artin asked (2026-09-05) about suggesting toys from a parent's store cookies and the answer is no, both technically (a static page cannot read another site's cookies) and by this rule. Nothing is ever uploaded; keep it that way. Any new stored key must be harmless if it leaks to nobody but the same browser.
4. **Green is the theme.** Palette tokens live in `styles.css` `:root`. Gold is for coins and highlights. Do not introduce a new primary color.
5. **Motion is a feature, not a bug.** Animations, confetti, sound blips, and bouncy buttons are wanted. Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (already wired) and keep everything usable with animations off.
6. **Money math must be honest.** The Chill-o-Meter uses 7%/year growth labeled as a game assumption; keep any rate clearly labeled. Never present a game number as a promise.
7. **Copy style:** playful, short sentences, no em-dashes or en-dashes (use commas, ellipses, or a new sentence). Spell dollar figures like $50.

## Where things live
- `index.html`: hero, dictionary, Play (4 games), Build teaser, Watch, Chill Words, Parents. `app.js` is one IIFE with a block per feature. Add a new game as a new block plus a new `<article class="game">` card.
- `build.html` + `builder.css` + three scripts, loaded in this order: `builder-art.js` (window.CB_ART: `SCENES`, sticker factories `S`, grid pieces `P`, kit heroes `H`, `blockSVG`, `palSVG`; every factory is `(uniqueId, item)` and reads `item.color` / `item.label` / `item.pal`), `builder-catalog.js` (window.CB_CATALOG: `KITS`, `ITEMS` via `V` sticker / `E` emoji / `G` grid piece / `B` Snap Block, `DEF`, `JOBS`), then `builder.js` (the engine). Prices are small whole dollars; free items exist so a world is never empty. Selling refunds the full price, except things reopened from the gallery (`free: true`), which were refunded once already.
- **The economy is work, save, spend (Artin, 2026-09-05, two dictations).** First: "if they want to make money, they have to build things and work." Second, after playing: "It shouldn't cost money to add the features... make a difference between a job and building. So the kids can do the jobs, get the money, and then build whatever they want." So: Snap Blocks, Magnet Tiles, and EVERY snap-together structure piece (`G(...)` in the catalog: build parts, roads, coaster track, river channels, slides, castle walls, garage decks, zoo bars, space tubes) are FREE and must stay free; everything else costs bucks. Money enters only through work: the WORK mini-games in `jobs.js` (seven of them, repeatable after a 45 s rest, each a real 20 to 60 second task; the Room Vacuum is the finger-driven one Artin asked for), build orders (`world.jobsDone`, once per world), and 🏆 Finish (`world.finished`, once per world, saves to `cb:worlds`). Work pays in BILLS (`Wallet.earnBill`), never straight into the wallet; a bill becomes spendable only when the kid drags it into a piggy (`Wallet.depositBill`, the Builder's deposit sheet or the home-page piggy tray). Denominations climb only with more work (`billForWork` in builder.js: $5, $20 every 5th, $100 every 20th, fat stack at 100, pot of gold at 250). Never add a way to earn that can be farmed (per-piece pay, instant repeats, refunding gallery items) and never let a job pay straight to the wallet.
- **One-tap goals.** `index.html?text=…` (or `?add=…&title=…`) opens the grown-up form prefilled from a store app's Share sheet: `manifest.webmanifest` declares a `share_target` (Android, installed PWA) and the parents' card documents the iPhone Shortcut recipe. `sw.js` exists only to make the page installable; it caches nothing. The site still reads nothing from any other site; the grown-up chooses to share a link.
- **The piggy tray** shows $1 coins (free, unlimited) plus the earned bills. Savings goals come from `goals.js` plus the grown-up form (`cb:goals`) as picture cards (a kid who cannot read picks by picture); reaching one shows a check, a certificate maker (canvas PNG, `makeCertificate` in app.js), and the grown-up store button. Do not hardcode goals in `app.js` again.
- **Snap Blocks and Magnet Tiles are ours.** Studded 3D-look blocks (`blockSVG`: front face on the grid, top and side faces pushed back by DEPTH, `def.snapTop` tells the engine how much of the art sits above the front face) and translucent magnet tiles (`tileSVG`) in ten colors that snap to a 24-cell grid. Do not use any toy brand's name, logo, minifigure shape, or exact brick or tile proportions anywhere in code, copy, or art. The pal (character) is a rounded cartoon figure on purpose, not a minifigure.
- **Characters and vehicles are homages, never copies.** `Z.*` (27 characters and animals) and `VH.*` (14 vehicles) in builder-art.js capture a silhouette, a palette, and a size, with our own names and details (Dash, Midnight, Spongy, Zappy, Sky Racer coupe, Rocket coupe, Boxy classic...). Never add a real character's name, logo, or exact costume; never add a car badge. Each character keeps its own `color` default on the shelf; the shop color row only drives the material kits (`MATERIAL_KITS` in builder.js).
- **Pictures are the realistic look.** `sprites.js` lists PNG or WebP pictures (transparent background) in `assets/sprites/`; a sprite whose id matches a drawn thing REPLACES that drawing everywhere (shelf, saved worlds, orders), a new id becomes a new thing. Pictures face right; the motion engine flips them. Give each an `ar` (height over width) so it lays out at the right shape before loading. Keep each file small (crop, 1200 px wide, WebP, under about 200 KB) and never reference a picture that is not committed. Pictures are homages with our own names: no badges, logos, or brand names, same rule as the drawings. The five vehicles (skyRacer, redRocket, boxyClassic, monsterTruck, semiTruck) are pictures as of 2026-09-05; `SPRITE_PACK.md` holds the brand-free prompts and the camera they set (straight side view). Backdrops work the same way: `CHILLION_BACKDROPS` in `sprites.js` lists pictures in `assets/backdrops/` (3:2, empty level ground in the bottom 40%, straight side view); a backdrop with a drawn scene's id replaces that scene, bottom-anchored cover, drawn in snapshots too.
- Item state: `{uid, kind, x, y, s, flip, rot, z, color, label, pal, free}`; x/y are fractions of the items layer (`#items`, which sits inside the stage border; all geometry uses its rect). `snapItem` aligns left/top edges to the grid, with the stud row of a block poking above the line.
- `sfx.js` (sounds) and `wallet.js` (shared bucks) load on both pages before the page script.
- `videos.js` is the only content file a non-coder edits.
- The piggy game is drag-and-drop by design (Artin, 2026-09-05: "the kid has to drag the money or coin into the piggy bank"). Keyboard users can press Enter on a coin. Do not turn it back into tap-to-add.

## Testing
No test framework. Before pushing: `node --check app.js builder.js builder-art.js builder-catalog.js jobs.js sfx.js wallet.js goals.js sprites.js`, `node verify-videos.mjs`, then open `index.html` and `build.html` in a browser at desktop and phone widths and click through every game once (tap a goal card, drag a coin into the piggy, answer a Need or Want card and tap the button twice fast, place a free block and drag it so it snaps, copy and turn it, place a magnet tile and a slide, paint a river with the 🌊 brush, open 💼 Jobs, do the Pool Cleaner, drag the bill into the piggy on the deposit sheet, buy a character and a vehicle, tap 🏆 Finish, reopen the world from 📚 My worlds, make a pal, save a picture). Watch the console: an SVG attribute error means a factory string is malformed. Playwright drives of all of this live in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.
