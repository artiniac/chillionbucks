Two additional owner-supplied recordings are included in full:

- drive-gh014025.mp3: AudioTranscriberAI_GH014025.mp3, 244.98 seconds.
- drive-img0647.mp3: AudioTranscriberAI_IMG_0647.mp3, 60.58 seconds.

MP3 audio frames were copied without re-encoding; nonessential source metadata was removed. No internal cuts, loop slicing, time stretching, pitch changes, or silence gating. Source shifts and throttle lifts remain intact. Local Whisper speech detection returned no speech segments in either new file; this is not an audible listening review.

All six tracks advance automatically at their ends, independent of laps, cornering, and throttle. Pause, mute, and leaving the tab pause the same position; returning resumes it. After the last file, the playlist returns to the first. A single media element streams audio, and the next compressed file is prefetched. This avoids decoding the full five-plus minutes into RAM on mobile. A media handoff may have a short browser-dependent gap; it does not wait for a new lap or acceleration.

Per-recording attenuation approximately matches the louder clips to -18 LUFS without raising the two quieter original clips.
