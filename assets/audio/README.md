# Quiet water park ambience

Three twenty-second clips generated for this project with Higgsfield Mirelo Text to Audio, September 8, 2026:

1. 5b81b1ec-0d13-442b-9618-155692fd1c49: gentle lapping and bubbling water, very distant chatter.
2. 0b182095-9e8c-4ff0-b3d1-0b151eb5ef53: quiet fountain, ripples, and subdued crowd ambience.
3. fe496b89-d6b9-4ad9-84df-3d5218923865: shaded lazy river, soft splashes, and faint background murmur.

These are generated environmental sounds, not recordings of identified children. Peaks were compressed and clip edges faded before MP3 export. Playback volume is 0.09, down from the rejected initial clip's 0.22 setting. Clips are shuffled without immediate repeats, with randomized gaps between 3.5 and 12 seconds. The existing water sound continues during gaps.

Playback starts after user interaction, follows the shared mute control, and pauses when hidden. The original louder crowd clip is not shipped.
