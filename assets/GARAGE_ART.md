# Car construction artwork

`garage-workshop.webp` is original workshop artwork generated with the connected Higgsfield account on September 17, 2026, using `gpt_image_2_5`. Generation ID: `da1a9a10-5353-4910-a4de-eaec2bdaa827`.

It appears above the desktop town Cars catalog. Phones use a compact tray with rendered model thumbnails to preserve play space.

The four interactive assembly models are original procedural geometry in `town-cars.js`. They are simplified interpretations of the requested Ferrari F430, Lamborghini Aventador, Nissan Skyline R34, and BMW E30. They are not 3D scans or manufacturer engineering models. No manufacturer badges are used. The ten educational assembly stages represent chassis, subframes, suspension and brakes, engine, transmission and drivetrain, interior, body, wheels and tires, windows, and lights.
