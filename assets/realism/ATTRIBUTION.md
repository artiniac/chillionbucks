# Captured lighting and surface materials

Downloaded September 7, 2026, from Poly Haven. These assets are CC0, free for commercial use without payment. The game serves committed local files and does not call Poly Haven's API during play.

Source and license: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky): https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
- Studio Small 08: https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_08
- Coast Sand 01: https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01
- Concrete Pavement: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_pavement
- Leafy Grass: https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass

Files are the 1K HDR environment maps and 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness maps. Each downloaded file was checked against the MD5 checksum in the provider's asset manifest. Powered by Poly Haven.

The Three.js RGBELoader and Water helpers are vendored from the official r170 examples, under the existing MIT license at vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt. Imports were adapted for local loading, and Water exposes its reflection target cleanup through disposeReflection.
