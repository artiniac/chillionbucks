# Visual reference research

These are references for original procedural artwork and short educational labels. Animal and building shapes are original procedural artwork. The later rendering pass uses CC0 captured lighting and scanned surfaces; see assets/realism/ATTRIBUTION.md. No third-party animal or building models were copied. Game bucks, animal sizes, compatibility, and care timing are not real husbandry advice.

## Aquarium

- [Georgia Aquarium, palette surgeonfish](https://www.georgiaaquarium.org/animal/palette-surgeonfish/): blue body, dark markings, and yellow tail. The model uses Paracanthurus hepatus, not the different Atlantic species also called a blue tang.
- [Aquarium of the Pacific, palette surgeonfish](https://www.aquariumofpacific.org/onlinelearningcenter/species/palette_surgeonfish): oval profile and coloration.
- [Tennessee Aquarium, clownfish and anemones](https://tnaqua.org/app/uploads/2020/06/Clownfish__Anemones.pdf): Amphiprion ocellaris.
- [National Aquarium, yellow tang](https://aqua.org/explore/animals/yellow-tang): Zebrasoma flavescens.
- [Two Oceans Aquarium, copperband butterflyfish](https://www.aquarium.co.za/animals/copperband-butterflyfish): Chelmon rostratus, distinctive bands and snout.
- [NOAA repository, Gramma loreto](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/12675/noaa_12675_DS1.pdf): purple anterior body, yellow posterior body and tail.
- [Aquarium of the Pacific visitor guide](https://www.aquariumofpacific.org/downloads/2024VisitorGuideFrogsENG.pdf): Chromis viridis identification.

Color varieties of crayfish are labeled as freshwater varieties. Generic crab, lobster, plant, and decoration models are not assigned invented species names. The collection remains an imaginative mixed habitat, with this distinction explained to grown-ups.

## Architecture references for the next town expansion

- [National Park Service, Modern Architecture](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/architecture/modern.htm).
- [National Park Service, Low, Light and Livable](https://home.nps.gov/articles/000/low-light-and-livable-from-modern-to-ranch-in-arkansas-1945-1970.htm): low profiles, light, and postwar residential design.
- [National Park Service, Colonial Revival](https://home.nps.gov/articles/colonial-revival-architecture.htm): historical revival of Georgian and Federal design.
- [National Park Service, Wright World Heritage architecture](https://home.nps.gov/articles/000/the-20th-century-architecture-of-frank-lloyd-wright-world-heritage-site.htm): horizontal composition and flowing interior spaces.

The game should translate characteristic massing, roofs, materials, glazing, and landscape relationships into original buildings. These are style-informed fictional homes, not replicas of specific private properties.
