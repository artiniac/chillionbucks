About Coin Run

The runner moves forward on their own. Children steer left and right and jump. Tapping anywhere on the game also jumps. With Jump helper on, gaps and logs are jumped automatically, and an early jump over a gap gets one rescue hop. There are no enemies and no lives. Falling into the water floats the runner back to the last star post in a bubble, and the next try at that gap is jumped for them. Springs and orange arrows give short boosts. The loop runs by itself.

Coins are play points for this run only. They never go into the wallet, and nothing here pays real bucks. The best coin count and stars for each speed are saved in this browser only, under cb:coinrun:v1. Leo and Dad use the family's photo-derived 3D heads that the other games already use; Buddy is an original character. No names, photos, or data leave the device.

For less motion, choose Easy. Sound can be turned off with the speaker button.
