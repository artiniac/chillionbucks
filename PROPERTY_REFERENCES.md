# Town property references

Reviewed September 17, 2026. These are simplified, interactive game models, not measured surveys, construction documents, or exact digital replicas. All five homes appear under Leo's homes. Existing saved placements are preserved.

## 20171 Allentown Drive, Woodland Hills

The model uses the previously reviewed public floor plan and aerial images, plus the owner's supplied backyard video. The video informed the rear glazing, dark slatted patio cover, paving with grass joints, pool and spa, play equipment, and garden. This release retains that geometry.

LADBS PRISM also lists the original 1961 permit and later additions. The reviewed 1997 permit describes an irregular bedroom/bathroom addition and work in the existing house. It does not establish a complete current floor plan on its own.

## Source handling

City documents and third-party listing photographs were inspected as references, not incorporated as image textures or redistributed with the game. Owner photos and videos remain outside the public repository. Construction lessons show simplified assemblies and do not claim to reproduce each property's hidden structural, plumbing, electrical, or mechanical systems.
